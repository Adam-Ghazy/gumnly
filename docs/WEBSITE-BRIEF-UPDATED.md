## Company positioning

GUMNLY Tech is a **SaaS-first technology startup** developing its own digital products. It also accepts selected **custom software development**, workflow automation and system integration projects. Products are the core business; services are an additional line, not the entire company identity.

Suggested descriptor: **SaaS Products & Software Engineering**.

## Website goals

1. Explain what GUMNLY is and the products it is building.
2. Give honest product status and show verified screenshots or explicitly labelled conceptual demos.
3. Convert product interest into demo/early-access conversations, where genuinely available.
4. Convert qualified custom software enquiries into discovery conversations.

Do not promise an available demo, live SaaS access, features, customers or measurable outcomes unless verified.

## Navigation and pages

- **Home:** company positioning, products first, capabilities, custom development, process, contact CTA.
- **Products:** first SaaS product; clearly state `In development` until launched. Product name and detailed scope are provisional.
- **Services:** custom software, workflow automation, system integration, each with concrete inputs/outputs and realistic delivery approach.
- **About:** founder-led story and only verified factual experience.
- **Contact:** working form with validation, spam/rate limiting and verified delivery destination.
- **Privacy:** actual data-handling disclosure completed before public form launch.

Solutions/use cases may be subsections within Products/Services at first; split into separate pages when substantive content exists.

## Homepage sequence

1. **Hero:** company-level SaaS-first positioning, clear CTA to Products, secondary CTA for Services.
2. **Our Products:** first SaaS product, actual development status, supported use cases and honest demo disclaimer.
3. **What We Build:** SaaS product engineering, automation and integrations.
4. **Custom Development:** concise description and discovery CTA.
5. **How We Work:** discovery → design → development → review/iteration, without promising a fixed process for all projects.
6. **Contact:** distinct product-interest and project-enquiry paths, with functional delivery.

## Brand and design

Follow `GUMNLY-BRAND-KIT.md`:

- Warm Neutral `#EDE8DF` ~70% visual area
- Teal Green `#0F8F73` ~25%
- Deep Green `#0B2F2B` ~5%
- **Three brand colours only; no gradients.**
- Manrope headings, Inter body.
- Solid colours, generous spacing, precise editorial hierarchy, custom workflow illustrations.
- Accessible focus states, contrast verification, keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support.

## Technical approach

- Next.js + TypeScript; static-first marketing pages and minimal client-side JavaScript.
- No marketing-site application database unless actual requirements justify it.
- Centralized colour/type/spacing tokens, responsive assets and locally served licensed fonts.
- Semantic HTML, SEO metadata, sitemap, robots and accessible form labels.
- Contact submission: server validation, rate limiting/spam protection, verified delivery destination and truthful success/failure feedback.
- Email/DNS setup remains a separate deployment prerequisite, not a blocker for local development.
